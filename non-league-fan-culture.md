## Groundhopper Voices — The Ones That Stayed With Me

**Paul Gerald** found a man at Edgeley Park who'd been coming since 1948:
> *"Since me dad got back from the war... On that stand right here, only back then it weren't a stand, just a terrace."* — Game Day Stories, Jan 2026

**Matt the Groundhopper** (24 NL South grounds, 18 to go):
> *"Many of these places haven't been upgraded in years. Rather than being a drawback, it gives them character — a throwback feel that takes me back to 1970s and 80s footage."* — Groundhopper Weekly, Sep 2025

**Alex Irons** (Glory Hunter):
> *"I can't watch Fulham these days — I'm often outpriced. I've relied on non-league football more and more."* — Game Day Stories

**Matt Coughlan** (Turncoat author):
> *"I don't feel like I've ever felt like I've wasted a journey anywhere — random places like Redbridge, Biggleswade... quite good to explore through the medium of football."* — Game Day Stories, Jan 2026
